HelmChat is a voice intercom for motorcyclists. It is an audio-only group
calling app built on the Jitsi Meet SDK, with an interface designed to be
operated with gloves on — at speed.

Video streams are strictly disabled and the Opus codec is capped at 24 kbps to
keep data usage and battery drain low. A high-contrast dark theme, a 168 dp mic
toggle, haptic feedback and an ongoing call notification make it usable on a
bike. Bluetooth SCO/HFP routing sends call audio to a helmet intercom, with a
dedicated button to switch between the headset and the phone speaker.

You can join or create a room on curated public Jitsi servers, use your own
self-hosted server, or supply a JWT token for authenticated deployments. Recent
rooms are remembered, and an invite can be shared as a plain browser link.
The app offers 30 languages, per-app language selection on Android 13+, and an
adaptive layout for phones, tablets and foldables.

This build uses Jitsi Meet SDK 13.1.1 compiled from source with LIBRE_BUILD=true.
No prebuilt Jitsi AAR is consumed, and the release dependency graph contains no
Play Services, Firebase, analytics or install-referrer libraries. The Jitsi JS
bundle is patched at build time to shorten the conference reload countdown to
about five seconds for motorcycle connections.
